# With thanks

Hearth is not built alone. It rests on the work of people who chose to share
what they made, often for free, often quietly, so that the rest of us could
stand a little higher.

These are those people. Where we can name them, we name them, and we link to
their own work so you can meet them on their terms. Leads are marked, because
someone usually carries the heaviest part of the load. Bots are left out, since
they are not people. Everyone here is a person whose hands shaped something
Hearth depends on.

This is the part of the project that matters most to us. Not the code. The care
that other people put in first, and the trust they extended by giving it away.

---

## The foundations underneath

These are the deep layers, the runtimes and libraries that everything above
quietly leans on. Each one keeps its own gallery of thanks, and we point you to
those rather than try to copy thousands of names here. Go read them. They are
worth it.

- **Python**, by the Python Software Foundation. The language at the bottom of
  most of this. Its own acknowledgments live in CPython's ACKS file:
  [github.com/python/cpython/blob/main/Misc/ACKS](https://github.com/python/cpython/blob/main/Misc/ACKS)
- **uv**, by Astral. The fast, friendly installer that sets the tools up cleanly:
  [github.com/astral-sh/uv/graphs/contributors](https://github.com/astral-sh/uv/graphs/contributors)
- **Chroma**. The memory store that lets mempalace search by meaning:
  [github.com/chroma-core/chroma/graphs/contributors](https://github.com/chroma-core/chroma/graphs/contributors)
- **Hugging Face**, and the transformers community. The models and tooling for
  understanding language:
  [github.com/huggingface/transformers/graphs/contributors](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/graphs/contributors)
- **NumPy**. The numerical foundation under so much of the rest, with its own
  thank-you record:
  [github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/main/THANKS.txt](https://github.com/numpy/numpy/blob/main/THANKS.txt)
