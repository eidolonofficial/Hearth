READ ME FIRST (macOS)


Welcome. You are in the right place, and there is nothing here you can get wrong.

This is the macOS version of Hearth. It does exactly what the Windows one does,
with Mac-friendly steps. Everything that matters, the warmth, the safety, the
"nothing happens without your yes," is the same.


HOW TO START

Find the app named "Hearth" in this folder and double-click it. A window opens
and walks you through everything in plain words. No commands to type, nothing
technical to know.

Prefer plain text over a window? "Welcome.command" in this folder does the exact
same thing in a Terminal window. Either one is fine.


IF MACOS WILL NOT OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME

macOS is careful with apps that came from somewhere else, so the very first time
it may refuse to open this one. That is normal, and it is not a problem. Do this
once: right-click (or hold Control and click) "Hearth", choose Open, then click
Open again in the box that appears. After that first time, a normal double-click
works.

If you are using "Welcome.command" instead and it will not run, open the Terminal
app, type  chmod +x  followed by a space, drag "Welcome.command" onto the
Terminal window, and press Enter. Then double-click it as usual.

You are safe the whole way. Nothing happens without your yes, you can close the
window any time, and it cannot harm your computer.


WHAT IT DOES

The same as the main READ ME FIRST.txt one folder up. It sets up your two skills,
Setup and Eidolon, right away, then offers a couple of optional helpers one at a
time. Each helper, if you say yes, is fetched from its maker's own official
source, out in the open. That file also explains why you want both skills, and
it previews the one question Setup asks the first time: how familiar you are with
coding and engineering, where there is no wrong answer.

On a Mac, the optional helpers install through a small tool called uv. If uv is
not on your computer yet, Hearth shows you the one official command to add it and
then stops, so you stay in charge.
