READ ME FIRST


Welcome. You are in the right place, and there is nothing here you can get wrong.


WHAT HEARTH IS

Hearth is a plain-language front door that sets Claude up for you, one step at a
time. You double-click one file; it does the rest, and it asks before every step.


YOU ARE SAFE

Nothing happens without your yes. Every real step pauses and asks first, and you
can answer yes, no, or maybe later. Close Hearth whenever you want and walk away.
It cannot harm your computer, and you cannot break anything by clicking around.
Stop halfway and that is fine too.


HOW TO START

On Windows, find the file named "Start Here" and double-click it. On a Mac, open
the "macos" folder and double-click the app named "Hearth" (the Mac copy of this
guide, macos/READ ME FIRST.txt, has the one extra first-open step). Either way,
a window opens and walks you through the rest in plain words. No commands to
type, nothing technical to know.


THE SHORT STORY (the five W's and How)

What:  Hearth sets up a small set of tools that help Claude remember your work
       and understand your projects. It asks before each step.

Why:   So you, running your own business, get a working setup without ever
       feeling lost or talked down to. You stay in charge the entire time.

Who:   Hearth was made by Jonah Butterbaugh, working alongside Claude. The
       makers of every other tool it mentions are thanked by name in CREDITS.md.

Where: Jonah's own tools come with Hearth, already included, along with one
       openly credited piece by others: the ASI-Evolve evolve engine by
       GAIR-NLP, carried inside Eidolon with its license and provenance in
       plain view. Any other tool made by someone else is never carried inside
       Hearth. Say yes to one and Hearth fetches it fresh from the maker's own
       official source, and only then.

When:  Whenever you are ready. There is no clock and no pressure. Come back
       later if you like.

How:   You double-click "Start Here" (on a Mac: the Hearth app in the macos
       folder). Hearth greets you, sets up the included
       tools, and then offers a few optional extras one at a time. For each one
       it shows a short, honest card explaining what it is, and waits for your
       yes, no, or maybe later.


WHAT GETS SET UP RIGHT AWAY, AND WHY BOTH

Two tools Jonah built himself come with Hearth and install at the very start, so
you see something work in the first few seconds. They do two different jobs, and
that is exactly why you want both.

  Setup    Gets a work session off to a strong start. When you sit down to a
           piece of work, it asks a few short questions about how you want to
           work today, then it remembers what worked and what broke, and
           double-checks its own claims before it says anything is done. The
           first time, it also asks how familiar you are with coding and
           engineering (new to this, some familiarity, comfortable I code, or
           expert), so Claude can meet you where you are. There is no wrong
           answer, and you can change it any time. Setup is about the session:
           this visit, this stretch of work.

  Eidolon  Reads a whole project folder and sets up everything Claude needs to
           understand it. When you want to build or change something, it carries
           that work from a plain idea all the way to finished: it plans, builds
           one careful piece at a time, checks for safety and mistakes, and only
           then puts it in place. It looks out for you the whole way, with guards
           that watch for risky moves, a safety review that hunts for ways a
           change could go wrong, and nothing important deleted or shipped
           without your clear yes. Eidolon is about the project: the place itself.

Here is why both, in plain terms. Picture your project as a workshop. Eidolon
gets the workshop ready, so Claude knows where every tool lives and works safely
in it. Setup is how you settle in for a good session once you are inside. With
only Eidolon, Claude understands the project but starts every session cold and
keeps forgetting how you like to work. With only Setup, each session is focused
but Claude never really learns the project. Together, the project is understood
and every session inside it is remembered and checked. They even hand off to
each other: Setup passes your answers straight to Eidolon, so the two run as a
pair.

These are Jonah's own work, with one credited exception carried inside Eidolon:
the ASI-Evolve evolve engine by GAIR-NLP, included in plain view with its
license and provenance. Everything goes in openly, with nothing hidden, and the
install itself needs no extra runtimes or downloads.


WHAT YOU CAN ADD, ONLY IF YOU WANT

After the first win, Hearth introduces two optional extras, one at a time. Skip
either one, now or forever. Nothing is ever added behind your back. Say yes and
Hearth installs it from the maker's own official site, in the open, telling you
exactly what is happening.

  graphify   Maps how the pieces of a project connect, so Claude can see the
             whole shape of your code. Made by Safi Shamsi.

  mempalace  Gives Claude a longer memory across sessions, like a filing cabinet
             it can search later. Led by a maker who goes by igorls, with many
             hands helping.

If one of these needs a small piece of background software to run, Hearth
explains it the same way, shows you the one command to set it up, and then stops
and lets you decide. It never installs that kind of thing behind your back.


IF SOMETHING DOES NOT GO THROUGH

Sometimes a step does not finish on the first try. That is normal, and it is not
your fault. When it happens, Hearth does not throw a scary red error. It tells
you, in plain words, the one thing to try, or you can skip it for now. You have
not broken anything, and your computer is fine.
